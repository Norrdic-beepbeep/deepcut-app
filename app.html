📋 Legal Protocol & Data Processing

1. The DeepCut Guarantee (Zero Retention)

DeepCut operates as a transient processing node. We do not want your proprietary media, and we do not keep it.

  • Media Files: Uploaded timelines, videos, and audio files are processed in volatile memory and are permanently purged the absolute millisecond your audit report is generated.
  • No Model Training: Transcripts and metadata routed through our third-party AI brokers are strictly ring-fenced. Your data is never used to train public or private AI models.

2. Data We Collect (UK GDPR Compliance)

To provide this service, we store only the absolute minimum required operator data:

  • Account Credentials: Your Name, Username, and heavily encrypted password.
  • Audit Logs: The text-based results of your compliance audits are saved to your secure Vault so you can review them later.

3. Terms of Service

  • System Limitations: DeepCut is a highly advanced AI-assisted tool, not a human legal team. Our reports should be used as an advisory layer. You remain ultimately responsible for the final compliance of your exported media.
