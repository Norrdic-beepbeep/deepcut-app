Why DeepCut?

DeepCut is designed to eliminate manual cinematic auditing errors. Our proprietary engine leverages advanced AI to instantly detect unauthorized copyrighted material, glaring continuity errors, and broadcast standards violations.

📋 Legal Terms & Protocol Integration

1. Processing Layer: DeepCut acts as an automated processing node parsing user-submitted structural timelines (XML) and spatial audio files.

2. AI Broker Routing: By registering, you understand transcript layers and text maps route securely into OpenAI core nodes via API parameters. Data remains strictly localized and is never trained on.

3. Data Sovereignty: Identity records are securely salted and hashed. You retain structural authority to purge user data routes and historical parameters securely at any time.
