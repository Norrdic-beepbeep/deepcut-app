Why DeepCut?

DeepCut is designed to eliminate the costly human errors associated with manual cinematic auditing. Before media goes live to broadcast or streaming, it must pass rigorous standards.

Our proprietary engine leverages advanced AI to instantly detect unauthorized copyrighted material, glaring continuity errors, and broadcast standards violations (such as profanity or explicit competitor brand mentions). With military-grade JWT encryption and a secure cloud architecture, your pre-release media remains strictly confidential.

How to operate

1

Input Timeline

Drag and drop a supported file into the main workspace. DeepCut explicitly requires raw structural timelines (XML, FCPXML, EDL) or direct raw media (MP4). You may also paste a direct web media URL.

2

Initialize Audit

Once media is verified, click the Initialize button. The system will securely hash signatures and poll security APIs before moving to the AI processing phase.

3

Review Findings

Upon completion, the engine will generate an official Compliance Audit Log. If the engine detects flags (e.g., unauthorized audio detected, jump-cut noted), they will be timestamped and classified for immediate correction.
